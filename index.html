Squarespace Domain DNS Guide

Follow these 3 simple steps to connect your domain www.bhoomisolutionsltd.com to your deployed web app:

Step 1: Log in to Squarespace Domains

Go to domains.squarespace.com and click on bhoomisolutionsltd.com -> DNS Settings.

Step 2: Add DNS Records

  • A Record: Host: @ | Points to: 76.76.21.21 (Vercel) or 75.2.60.5 (Netlify)
  • CNAME Record: Host: www | Points to: cname.vercel-dns.com (Vercel)

Step 3: Save & Verify

Save changes. HTTPS/SSL is automatically provisioned for free!
