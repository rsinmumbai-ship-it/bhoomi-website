Privacy Policy & Data Protection

DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, SOC2 & ABDM Compliance Guidelines

Last Updated: September 25, 2026

At Bhoomi Solutions Ltd (www.bhoomisolutionsltd.com), we prioritize the security, confidentiality, and data privacy of all clients, partners, and platform visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, encrypt, and protect your information.

1. Compliance Framework (DPDP Act 2023, GDPR & ABDM)

In accordance with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), SOC2 Type II guidelines, and Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) standards, Bhoomi Solutions operates on strict consent-driven data principles. Data processing is transparent, auditable, and restricted to stated enterprise automation objectives.

2. Information We Collect

We collect professional contact details provided directly by you when submitting our lead contact form or interacting with Bhoomi Online:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full Name, Work Email Address, and Phone Number (if provided).
  • Enterprise Context: Industry Sector, Automation Goals, and Project Requirements.
  • Technical Metadata: Anonymized session telemetry, browser type, and interaction event metrics for platform security.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is exclusively used to evaluate enterprise project feasibility, schedule custom Proof-of-Concept (PoC) demonstrations, and deliver requested AI services. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize client data with any third parties.

4. Data Encryption & Security Standards

All data transmitted to or from Bhoomi Solutions infrastructure is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Healthcare data integrations adhere to zero-retention FHIR standards aligned with ABDM protocols.

5. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR, you have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate records, or request complete deletion of your contact information from our communications registry.
